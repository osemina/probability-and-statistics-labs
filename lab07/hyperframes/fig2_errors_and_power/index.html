Two errors and the power of a right-tailed test
test statistic
What the areas mean
■ α: reject H₀ although it is true (Type I error)
■ β: keep H₀ although it is false (Type II error)
■ Power = 1 − β: correctly reject a false H₀
Smaller α (0.05 → 0.01) → critical value moves right → β grows, power falls.
