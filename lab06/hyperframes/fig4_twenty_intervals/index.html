20 samples, 20 different 95% intervals for μ = 50
true mean μ = 50
Each sample gives its own x̄ (gold) and its own interval x̄ ± 1.96·σ/√n.
Blue: the interval contains μ.
Red: the interval misses μ.
19 of 20 intervals contain μ, which is about 95%.
“95% confident” describes the method: in the long run, 95% of such intervals catch μ.
